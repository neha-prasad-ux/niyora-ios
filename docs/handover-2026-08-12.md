## IN-FLIGHT — the Moon reflection redesign (main next-session work)

Triggered by tester feedback: *"Moon feels great when right, throws you off when wrong."* Design principle agreed: **be wrong gracefully, not right more often.**

### 1. Feedback reactions (built, needs finishing)
Per-read **heart / no-reaction / cross** is BUILT (commit `6d91f9e`) but two things remain:
- **Wire it into the AI** (the prompt pass, below). `collectReactions()` gives `{liked, rejected}`; feed it to the reflect calls.
- **Neha verifies the visuals on device** (needs a build): icons are SF Symbols heart/heart.fill/xmark size 18, rose fill; reaction row sits BELOW each read; soft scale-settle on tap.

**Locked reaction semantics** (don't relitigate):
- ❤️ heart = **"yes"** — soft, "resonates" → steer WARMER, do NOT signal "done".
- no reaction = **still with it** (neutral, NOT a rejection — never steer away from an un-hearted read).
- ✗ cross = **"not this"** (not "dislike") → steer away + re-roll a genuinely different read.
- Absence is the free negative; the cross is the active "not me". No thumbs-down/grading language.

### 2. The prompt pass (do WITH Neha — she is reading the prompts)
All Moon prompts are in `src/lib/moment-gemini.ts` `SLOT_INSTRUCTION`. A readable export was generated at `docs/niyora-moon-prompts.html` equivalent (regenerate from the code if missing). Neha is reviewing which to change. Two shared changes for the **reads/guess cards** (the ones producing points she reacts to — reflect_signal, reflect_wise, reflect_reframe, etc.):
- **Feedback clause** (every reflect call): *"She liked these earlier reads: ⟨…⟩. She rejected: ⟨…⟩. Lean toward what she liked and away from what she rejected, but still bring one genuinely new angle, never just agree."* (Steer but don't become a yes-machine.)
- **On cross/re-roll: a genuinely DIFFERENT read, not a reworded one.**
- Per-card: make interpretive reads clearly **tentative** ("a maybe", offered) so a wrong read lands as "not quite" not "unseen".

### 3. Acknowledge removal (decided, not started — do this cleanly)
The `acknowledge` echo beat (repeating her words back) is being **removed** — "repetition doesn't help". Today it still runs but is auto-advanced/hidden (only visible via back-nav). **The feeling guess becomes the opener.** Touches: `src/v3/moment-flow.ts` (drop the `acknowledge` node, re-wire `intro/clarify → feelings`), `src/app/moment.tsx` (remove `startEcho`/`echoOk`/`echoFixing`/`modelEcho` machinery + the `case 'acknowledge'`), `src/lib/moment-gemini.ts` (drop the `acknowledge` SLOT_INSTRUCTION), and `moment-flow.test.ts`.
