# Irritability — level-by-level draft (rough, no visuals)

First emotion track, drafted end-to-end to feel the rhythm before any visual
design. Science anchored to `niyora-emotion-bank.md` (§1 Irritability + F1/F2/F3).
Mechanic per `niyora-pms-game-design.md` (best-fit-gentle, intensity = master
variable, binary read).

**Voice (locked):** every in-app line sounds like a frank, warm 30-year-old
Californian friend. **Specific over vague** ("breathe out for 6," not "breathe
deeply"). Simple words. Says the real thing, kindly. No jargon, no shame, no hype.
Think *"you're not broken — your brain's just cranking the volume,"* not *"your
neurobiology is amplifying affective reactivity."*

**Self-distancing:** she helps a friend, **Neha** (recurring character across
chapters, so a relationship builds). Reasoning about a friend is wiser and less
defended than reasoning about yourself. (Upgrade path if we ever capture her name:
shift to her own name in the third person — illeism, Kross.)

**Interaction variety is a design goal.** Every level uses a *different* way to
respond: swipe, tap, hold-to-preview, slider, hold-to-breathe, chip-assembly.

## The meter: "Riding the wave" (label logic)

The 7/10 becomes a **wave** — the water is how worked up she is, steadiness is the
skill. Label = a function of *skill band* **and** *cycle window*.

| Skill | Label |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | *Learning the water* |
| 3–4 | *Finding your footing* |
| 5–6 | *Riding the wave* |
| 7–8 | *Steady in the swell* |
| 9–10 | *Calm in the current* |

**In-window logic:** during her PMS window the water's higher, so a rough day
**never lowers the label** — it just adds *"…and the water's high this week."*
Rough water, not lost ground. (Wording's a sketch; `label = f(skillBand, inWindow)`
is the real proposal.)

## Chapter 1 · Irritability — six levels

### L1 · Know the water  — *swipe: true / myth*
**Job:** clear up irritability + the basics that crank it up (hunger, blood sugar,
sleep) and plant the long-exhale idea. This is prep for L2 — get the fundamentals
down before helping anyone.
**Sound:** "Real quick — let's clear up a few things about getting irritable. Swipe
right if it's true, left if it's BS."
**Ask:** a stack of statement cards. **Swipe right = true, left = myth.** Each swipe
flips to a one-line, plain-spoken reveal.
**Cards:**
1. "When PMS makes you snappy, something's wrong with your hormones." → **Myth.**
   *Nope. Your hormones are normal — your brain's just more sensitive to them right
   now. That's a real difference. You're not broken.* (VERIFIED, F3)
2. "Getting irritable before your period is common and normal." → **True.** *About
   half of women get this. You're in huge company.* (VERIFIED, F3)
3. "Being hungry makes you more irritable." → **True.** *Low blood sugar turns the
   volume up on everything. Hangry is real — eat something before you decide you
   hate everyone.* (⚠ see gate)
4. "Bad sleep gives you a shorter fuse the next day." → **True.** *Less sleep, less
   patience. That's biology, not weakness.* (⚠ see gate)
5. "Breathing out longer than you breathe in helps you calm down." → **True.** *A
   long exhale literally tells your body to stand down. We'll do the real thing —
   Wind Down — later.* (MEDIUM — physiology; state softly)
**Close:** "So before you help anyone — check the basics. Is she hungry? Wiped out?
That's step one." (bridges to L2)
**⚠ Gate:** cards 3–4 are the practical drivers you asked to add. Keep them **general**
("being hungry makes you irritable") — that's defensible. Do **not** attach a
PMS-specific stat ("sleep doubles the odds"); that claim is [GAP]. Verify in the
interpersonal/physical-drivers research pass before adding numbers.

### L2 · Read the wave  — *binary tap: A little / A lot*
**Job:** teach the one read everything rests on — *how worked up is she* — because
that flips what helps. This level only trains the read; no response yet.
**Sound:** "Here's the thing that changes everything: how worked up is she — a
little, or a lot? Same friend, but what helps is totally different depending on the
answer. Let's practice spotting it."
**Ask:** read a short scene, tap one of two:
- **A little** — *annoyed, but she can still talk it through.*
- **A lot** — *past talking. Logic's not landing right now.*
Confirm + a plain why after each.
**Scenes:**
- A) "Maya's a little annoyed her partner didn't text back." → **A little.** *Bugged,
  but she can still think straight.*
- B) "Maya hasn't slept, her partner blew off something she worked really hard on,
  and her voice is shaking." → **A lot.** *She's flooded. Words aren't getting through
  right now.*
- C) (near-miss to sharpen it) "Maya's snapping at little stuff, but laughs when you
  point it out." → **A little.** *Still reachable.*
**Close:** "That's the whole skill — clock how worked up she is first. Nail that and
the rest is easy."
*(Label call: "A little / A lot" is my pick — dead simple and it names the exact
axis. Swappable for "Annoyed / Flooded" if you want more color.)*

### L3 · Match the move  — *pick a card; press-and-hold to preview its future*
**Sound:** "Cool — you can read it now. So pick what actually helps. Not sure? Hold a
card and you'll see how it plays out before you commit."
**Ask:** move cards. **Press-and-hold** any card to preview its "two futures";
release to step back; tap to commit. Gentle feedback — never "wrong."
**The flip — same three choices, opposite best-fit:**

*Scene 1 (A little):* "Maya's a little annoyed. What helps?"
- **Help her see it another way** ("he's slammed at work, not being a jerk") →
  *best-fit.* *When she's only a little worked up, talking it through actually
  works.* (F1)
- Give her space → *lesser.* *She didn't need space — you two could've just talked
  it out.*
- Tell her to push it down → *worst.* *That never works. It just comes back louder.*

*Scene 2 (A lot):* "Maya's flooded, running on no sleep. What helps?"
- Help her see it another way → *lesser now.* *Not right now. When she's this worked
  up, logic bounces off — sometimes it even stings.*
- **Give her space** ("20 minutes, let it pass, then talk") → *best-fit.* *You can't
  talk someone out of a flood. You wait for it to drop first.* (VERIFIED, F1)
- Tell her to push it down → *worst.* *Same deal — comes back louder.*
**Payload:** "Same three choices. The only thing that changed was how worked up she
was — and that flipped the right answer. That's the whole game." (Meter ticks up
most for getting *both* reads right.)

### L4 · Say it, or save it  — *slider on a timeline*  ⚠ content-gated
**Sound:** "Sometimes the annoyance is pointing at something real. The move isn't
swallowing it — it's saying it when she'll actually be heard."
**Ask:** a **slider** along a timeline. Drag to *when* Maya brings it up.
**Scene:** "Her partner genuinely blew off something she cared about. That's legit —
not 'just PMS.' When does she say something?"
- Left — **"Right now, mid-meltdown"** → *future: too flooded, it turns into a fight,
  nothing gets fixed.*
- Middle — **"Take 20, then say it straight"** → *best-fit: calm enough that it
  actually lands.*
- Right — **"Let it go, say nothing"** → *future: it doesn't disappear, it piles up.
  Swallowing it isn't the goal.*
**Ethical core (don't lose it):** best-fit is the *middle* — **say the real thing,
just not mid-flood.** Both ends cost her something, and "say nothing" is drawn as a
*bad* future so we never train self-silencing.
**⚠ Gate:** the F1 timing logic (wait out the flood) is fine now; the assertiveness
scaffolding (how to say it) is [GAP] — hold the finer copy for the interpersonal
research pass.

### L5 · Rehearse  — *press-and-hold to breathe*  (the hinge)
**Sound:** "Remember that long-exhale thing from before? Let's actually do it — this
is what 'give her space' feels like from the inside."
**Ask:** drop into the existing **Wind Down** technique — *deep relaxation*,
4-7-8 breathing (`id: 'wind-down'`, `techniques.ts`). **Press-and-hold** to breathe
in as the orb fills, hold, release for the long exhale. ~75s / 4 rounds, already
built.
**Teach:** "In for 4, hold 7, out for 8. The long exhale is the part that calms you
down — that's the whole trick." (Pays off L1 card #5; it's the app's `sedation`
motion + "match your breath" orb.)
**Why it's here:** L1–L4 are fast/heady; this is the one that lands in the body. And
it reuses a real asset — no new breath content to build.

### L6 · Your move next time  — *tap-to-assemble word chips (if-then)*
**Sound:** "Last thing — let's turn this into a plan for *you*, in your own words. So
it's ready before you need it."
**Ask:** build an if-then plan from **tappable chips** (mad-libs); some slots
free-type.
**Template:** *"When I'm **[a little / a lot]** worked up and **[someone blows me off
/ …]**, before I react I'll **[take 20 min / step outside / breathe out slow / say it
later]**."*
**Timing:** dated to her `pms-window`, resurfaces as a checklist nudge. Finishing it
in-app completes the level; actually *doing it* is a bonus (extra wave/soul
progress).

### Woven throughout · the kind word  — *press-and-hold*
Not a level — a beat that can close any session. **Press and hold** to give yourself
a break (fills like a slow breath).
**Sound:** "One kind thing to yourself. You're doing something hard here — that
counts." F2 self-compassion, countering the "why am I like this" spiral. Held, not
tapped — it should feel like a small act.
