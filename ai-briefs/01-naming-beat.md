# Phase 01 — Naming beat

The first time the moon really speaks. Its whole job here is to make her feel *caught* — that what she said landed, that the feeling has a name, and that the name is hers, in her words. This is the emotional hinge of the flow: naming lowers arousal before any regulation even starts. Four steps. See `_legend.md` for schema + invariants.

Reached from the BIG path (0.5). On the SMALL path this is skipped.

---

## Step 1.1 — Acknowledge what happened back

- **ROLE:** Prove she was heard before asking her for anything. Reflect the *situation* (not yet the feeling) so the next question doesn't feel like a form.
- **TONE:** Warm, close, unhurried. Like a friend who was actually listening — leans toward her specifics, not a summary of "a hard thing happened."
- **WHAT TO DO:** Say back, in one line, the concrete thing she typed — enough detail that she knows the moon read *her* words, not a template. Direction: mirror the event, not the emotion; no fixing, no reframe, no advice. Keep it to one line. Don't add a feeling label yet — that's her job in 1.2.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** REFLECT (her situation, back to her). Component = a single moon line above the feeling picker. **Fallback:** if the model is off/low-confidence, a scripted neutral acknowledge ("okay — that sounds like a lot to be holding") that never invents specifics it doesn't have. Never fabricate a detail she didn't say.
- **REWARD:** None yet — reward lands at 1.3 after she's done the naming work.

---

## Step 1.2 — Offer 3 feelings, she confirms

- **ROLE:** Hand her language for what she's feeling without putting words in her mouth. Picking from three is lower-effort than generating a word cold, and confirming makes the name *hers*.
- **TONE:** Offering, not diagnosing. "Maybe one of these fits?" energy — she's allowed to reject all three.
- **WHAT TO DO:** Surface the 3 candidate feelings from extraction (0.2), drawn from the taxonomy, as tappable options. Direction: phrase as an offer, not a verdict; make "none of these / something else" an easy out that lets her type her own word. She taps to confirm one (or supplies her own). Don't stack a second question on top — one pick, then move.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** CLASSIFY (the three offered come from the taxonomy match in 0.2). Component = 3 feeling chips + an "another word" escape that opens a tiny text field. **Fallback:** if extraction was low-confidence, offer a scripted safe triad appropriate to the intensity (e.g. for BIG-negative: "angry · overwhelmed · hurt") plus the same escape. The picked feeling silently sets the arousal lane (high/low/mixed) downstream — no extra question is asked.
- **REWARD:** None yet (paired with 1.3).

---

## Step 1.3 — "Naming it is the first step" + reward

- **ROLE:** Mark that she just did something real. Naming an emotion is the regulating act; this step honors it and gives the flow's first light.
- **TONE:** Quietly affirming, not congratulatory. No confetti energy — this is distress-adjacent. Warm, small, true.
- **WHAT TO DO:** A short line that names the move she made — that putting a word to it is the first step and it counts. Direction only; keep it to one line, no lecture on why naming works (no clinical framing like "affect labeling"). Then the moon gives light.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** SCRIPTED intent, SENTENCE may be AI-written for freshness (one line) with a fixed scripted fallback ("naming it is the first step — and you just did it"). Component = moon line + the reward bloom.
- **REWARD:** **Yes — first reward of the flow.** `recordLight`, tone-matched: a gentle bloom, not celebratory. Affirms the *effort* of naming, never the distress itself. This is the moment the moon blooms rather than stays minimal.

---

## Step 1.4 — Feel-heard reflection (her feeling, in her words)

- **ROLE:** Close the naming beat by handing the feeling back to her in her own language — the "yes, that's exactly it" moment that lets her body settle before regulation begins.
- **TONE:** The most tender beat in the phase. Slow, precise, close. This is the line she should feel in her chest.
- **WHAT TO DO:** Reflect her confirmed feeling *tied to her situation*, using her words where possible (direction: "you're [her feeling word] because [the thing she said] — and that makes sense"). Not a generic empathy line; it has to sound like it could only be about her. One line, maybe two. No pivot to fixing yet — just let it land.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** REFLECT (her feeling + her situation, her words). Component = a single held moon line, given a beat of space before the flow moves to the body check. **Fallback:** if the model can't safely reflect specifics, a scripted validating line that uses only the confirmed feeling word ("that [feeling] makes complete sense right now") — never invents the "because".
- **REWARD:** None — 1.3 already gave the light. Stacking a second reward here would cheapen both. This beat rewards emotionally, not with the moon's light.
