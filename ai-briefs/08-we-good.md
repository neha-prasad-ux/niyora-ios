# Phase 08 — We good?

The close. One honest check on whether she's actually okay to leave the flow — with a real branch if she's not, so "we good?" is a question, not a dismissal. See `_legend.md` for schema + invariants.

---

## Step 8.1 — Ask: we good?

- **ROLE:** Give her a genuine off-ramp *and* a genuine "no, I need more" — the flow doesn't assume it worked.
- **TONE:** Warm, unhurried, no pressure to say yes. The moon has time; she's not being wrapped up and shooed out.
- **WHAT TO DO:** Ask simply if she's feeling okay to leave it here (direction: plain, caring, one line). Make "not really" as easy to pick as "yeah". Don't pre-celebrate.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** SCRIPTED. Component = moon line + yes / not-yet control.
- **REWARD:** None here — the close reward (if any) lands on the actual exit (8.2).

---

## Step 8.2 — Yes → Close

- **ROLE:** Leave her cleanly, on a steady note, with the moon returning to minimal.
- **TONE:** Settled, warm, brief. Not a victory lap — a soft landing.
- **WHAT TO DO:** One short closing line (direction: acknowledge she showed up for herself; leave the door open to come back). The moon returns to its quiet resting state. No streak, no "see you tomorrow" nag.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** SCRIPTED intent; SENTENCE optional with fixed fallback ("you did the work here — I'm around whenever you need me"). Component = moon line, orb settles to minimal.
- **REWARD:** **Optional, small** — a final gentle `recordLight` for completing the flow is okay (affirming effort), but keep it quiet. Never a big finish; this is distress-adjacent work, not a level cleared.

---

## Step 8.3 — Not yet → more context + untried options

- **ROLE:** Take "no" seriously — gather what's still unsettled and offer something she *hasn't* already tried, rather than looping her back through the same beats.
- **TONE:** Patient, steady, still fully present. "Okay — tell me more" energy. No frustration that the flow didn't land.
- **WHAT TO DO:** Ask for a bit more context via an open text field (direction: what's still sitting wrong?). Read it, then suggest options she hasn't tried yet — a different Act 2 module, another regulation rung (the 3.9 ladder), or a fresh timing. Direction: explicitly avoid re-offering what she already did; name that there's more than one way through. Loop back to the relevant phase, then re-check at 8.1.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** CLASSIFY (map her added context → an untried module/rung) + REFLECT her added context back so she feels heard again. Component = text field + suggested-options buttons. **Fallback:** if the model can't route, offer a scripted short list of common untried options (a different module, the next regulation rung, hold-and-revisit). Never dead-end her.
- **REWARD:** None here — rewards resume inside whichever phase she re-enters. Don't reward the act of saying "not yet".
