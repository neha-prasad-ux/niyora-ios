# Phase 07 — Time it

She's picked how she'll handle it (Act 2). Before she acts, help her land it at the *right moment* — some things are best done today, some are worth waiting for a calmer/higher point in her cycle. This is where a handling choice becomes a concrete, well-timed action. See `_legend.md` for schema + invariants.

---

## Step 7.1 — "Tell her why timing matters"

- **ROLE:** Give her the one reason timing changes the outcome, so the wait (or the go-now) feels like strategy, not stalling.
- **TONE:** Practical, on-her-side coach. Brief — one plain line of the mechanism, not a lecture.
- **WHAT TO DO:** One science-context line on why *when* she does this matters (direction: hard conversations land better when both nervous systems are settled; an ask lands better at a higher-energy point). Non-clinical, ≤1 line. Framing rule from Gate 1 still holds — if it's a normal day, don't attribute timing to her cycle.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** SCRIPTED (vetted science line, no invented facts). Component = moon line.
- **REWARD:** None yet.

---

## Step 7.2 — Today vs unlocks ~day 14

- **ROLE:** Sort the action into "do it today" or "wait for the better window", and set the reveal so a waited-for action resurfaces at the right time.
- **TONE:** Clear and reassuring — waiting is an active, smart choice, not avoidance.
- **WHAT TO DO:** Offer the timing choice: **Today** (do it now / soon) or **hold until it unlocks ~day 14** (the higher-energy point in her cycle). Direction: present both as valid; never pressure the confrontation *now*. Only offer the day-14 unlock when cycle framing is ON (Gate 1) — on a normal day, offer a neutral "later, when you're steadier" hold instead of a cycle-anchored one.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** SCRIPTED. Component = two-way choice (Today / hold-until-unlock) that schedules the reveal for the held path. **Fallback:** none — deterministic.
- **REWARD:** None yet (paired with 7.3).

---

## Step 7.3 — Today action + "checklist for after the fight"

- **ROLE:** Persist the decision as something she'll actually see and do — a Today action if now, and where relevant a **checklist for after the fight** so she's not left raw with no plan.
- **TONE:** Warm, closing-the-loop. She's leaving with a real, doable thing, not just a feeling.
- **WHAT TO DO:** Write the chosen action to Today (direction: name it in her words / as her choice). Where the situation was a conflict, attach a short **"after the fight" checklist** (reconnect, self-care, what to watch for). Then reward. If held until day 14, confirm it'll come back then — no action written for today.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** SCRIPTED action write; the after-fight checklist is scripted content. Component = Today-action write + optional checklist + reward bloom. **Fallback:** none.
- **REWARD:** **Yes** — `recordLight`, warm, affirming she turned a feeling into a timed plan. Then → We good? (08).
