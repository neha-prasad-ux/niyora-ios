# Phase 04 — Low-arousal lane (flat / numb / drained)

Routed here **silently** from her 1.2 feeling. Her system is *under*-aroused, not flooded — so the whole logic inverts: **no breathing, no break, no calming.** That would sink her further. This lane is gentle *activation* — one tiny sensory input to lift her a notch. The bar is deliberately on the floor: doing *one* thing is a win. See `_legend.md` for schema + invariants.

---

## Step 4.1 — Offer activation activities (buttons)

- **ROLE:** Give her one small, sensory, low-effort thing that nudges the body upward — not a to-do list, not a mood project.
- **TONE:** Softest in the whole flow. Slow, unhurried, zero pressure. When someone's flat, brightness feels like a demand — so the moon stays quiet and warm, never peppy or cheerleading.
- **WHAT TO DO:** Offer a few tiny activation options as buttons: **sunlight · one song you love · a warm drink · pet the dog** (or her equivalent). Direction: each is one sensory hit, seconds to start; frame as an invitation, not homework. Explicitly *do not* run breathing or a timed break here — this is activation, not calming.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** SCRIPTED (fixed option set). Component = activity buttons, one tap picks one. No model needed. **Fallback:** none.
- **REWARD:** None yet — reward lands after she picks (4.3).

---

## Step 4.2 — "Just the one, done is enough"

- **ROLE:** Pre-empt the flat-state trap where more than one step feels impossible and she does nothing. Lower the bar out loud so the single action feels complete.
- **TONE:** Reassuring, permission-giving. "You don't have to do it all."
- **WHAT TO DO:** Tell her just one is the whole assignment — done is enough (direction; intent fixed). One line. No stacking, no "and then you could also…".
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** SCRIPTED intent; SENTENCE optional with fixed fallback ("just the one — done is enough"). Component = moon line shown with/after the buttons.
- **REWARD:** None yet (paired with 4.3).

---

## Step 4.3 — She does one → reward

- **ROLE:** Honor the activation. For a flat state, choosing to do a single small thing *is* the regulation — reward it fully.
- **TONE:** Warm, low, genuinely pleased-for-her without being loud. Match her energy up by a notch, don't overshoot it.
- **WHAT TO DO:** When she's picked/done one, give light and affirm the effort (direction: name that she moved at all). Don't ask for a second.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** SCRIPTED. Component = reward bloom + one moon line.
- **REWARD:** **Yes** — `recordLight`, gentle and warm. Affirms doing one small thing from a flat place, which is genuinely hard.

---

## Step 4.4 — "Feeling better?" → branch

- **ROLE:** Light check on whether the nudge lifted her, and the fork to finishing or exploring further.
- **TONE:** Soft, no pressure to perform improvement.
- **WHAT TO DO:** Ask if she's feeling a little better. **Yes / a bit** → hand off to Act 2 (explore how she handles it) or Close. **Not really** → offer another activation option (loop back to 4.1 with a different one) — still **no breathing, no break**. Direction: keep the ceiling low; even "a tiny bit" counts.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** SCRIPTED. Component = yes/no-ish picker. Yes → Act 2 (06). No → re-offer a different 4.1 activity.
- **REWARD:** None here — 4.3 already gave the light. Don't double-reward the check.
