### 🔴 TWO ACTS CARRY A SAFETY PRECONDITION (Neha, 2026-07-25)

**G · tell one person** — *"we have to say tell one person that you recognise
provides you a safe space."*
Correct, and the evidence adds a second requirement that matters more.
**Nils & Rimé: recovery happened EXCLUSIVELY when the listener stimulated
cognitive work.** Purely empathic listening produced closeness, less loneliness,
and *an impression of feeling better* — with no actual recovery. So "someone who
gives me a safe space" reads to most people as *someone who'll take my side*,
which is precisely the listener who produced the impression without the recovery.
Both requirements, safe first:
> **"pick someone you feel safe with who'll actually think it through with you,
> not just agree with you."**

**A · say it to them** — *"say it to them if you strongly believe you have safe
space with them."*
Right, **as a second layer — never as the gate.**
🔴 THIS DOES NOT REPLACE THE SAFETY SCREEN. When there are signs of coercive
control the option is **never generated at all** — not softened, not caveated.
By the time "say it to him directly" is on screen and we ask "do you feel safe
with him?", the idea is already in the room. And coercive control distorts
exactly that self-assessment: a woman being controlled may genuinely believe she
is safe, or may be unable to say otherwise, so **her answer is the least reliable
input at the precise moment we would be relying on it.**
Two independent layers:
  1. the screen decides whether she ever SEES the option (upstream, not her call)
  2. this line lets her decline it even when the screen allowed it
> **"only if you'd feel safe saying it to them."**

### Why this set CLOSES (the argument that makes "fully prepped" reachable)
An act can only aim at one of **five targets**: the other person · the situation ·
a third party · myself · my safety. The interpersonal ones are further bounded by
speech-act types. **Topics fan out forever; targets don't.** A new situation needs
new copy, never a new act.

### The ladder rule (how the anti-pessimism constraint becomes structural)
The three chips are ALWAYS **direct → preparatory → self-directed**.
Rung 3 is always present. Never three confrontational options. "Look after
myself" is a valid backstop for every situation in the document.
→ and see the evidence table below: **the best-evidenced acts are all low-effort
and non-confrontational**, so the ladder happens to put the strongest act on
every screen. The safe design and the evidenced design are the same design.

## 🔴 THE MOST DANGEROUS GAP — the generator, not the missing act

Act M being absent is a failure to protect. **The unscreened option generator is
worse, because it actively PRODUCES the harmful instruction:**

> the more dangerous her partner, the more likely "say it to him directly" gets
> generated

— because an abusive situation produces exactly the vent that reads as ordinary
interpersonal conflict.

**Two shipped decisions compound it:**
1. **the transcript persists by design** (Neha's decision, 2026-07-24) — a written
   record on a device he may control
2. we ship verbatim **"don't text him for 20 minutes"** into a home we know
   nothing about

**Screening the module while leaving the generator unscreened fixes nothing.**
This is the item that must be closed before any woman uses this.

## Coverage holes the draft-7 taxonomy could not reach

1. **"nobody to act on"** — imposter syndrome, doom-scroll, parenting guilt,
   jealousy, body image, replayed interactions. Only surviving act was "accept",
   which cannot even fill a three-chip UI.
2. **"the other person is gone"** — ghosted, breakup, grief, estrangement. The act
   she *wants* is exactly the one that must not be offered.
3. **institutional** — bias, harassment, health, money. Draft offered "set a
   boundary", which is useless and quietly dangerous when the correct act is a
   record plus a specialist.
4. **load** — overwhelm, caregiving, mental load. Structurally absent, and
   arguably the single most common women's situation.

## Acts carrying NO real evidence

Shipping an unevidenced act is fine. **Attaching a science line to it is not.**

- **hold a line** — boundary-setting and grey rock have **zero trials**
- **say it to them** — the DESC script has never been tested; it comes from a
  **1976 self-help book**. Soft-startup is observational only.
- **tell one person** — the loneliness meta's winning strategy was social
  *cognition* (4 RCTs); reaching out is not established as the active ingredient
- **find out** — the only act with a documented **iatrogenic** signal: 31–38%
  feel *worse* after health-related internet use
- **expressive writing** — r ≈ .075
- **"self-soothe" as a category** — no dismantling trial exists

**Robust elsewhere but NOT for wellbeing:** *ask for what I want* (strong evidence
that people say yes more than she expects — nothing about how she'll feel) and
*own my part* (vignette-based, recipient-rated).
