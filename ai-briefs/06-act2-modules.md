# Phase 06 — Act 2: "let's explore how you handle this"

The body's regulated (03/04/05) or the emotion was small (0.5). Now the flow turns from *feeling* to *handling* — a fan-out where the module is chosen by the situation/feeling. Every module shares one shape: **give the science context, then hand her a component to do the work.** Modules that produce a message: she **always edits + sends, never auto-send.** See `_legend.md` for schema + invariants.

---

## 6.0 — The shared Act 2 pattern (applies to every module below)

- **ROLE:** Move her from "I feel this" to "here's how I handle it well" — one situation-matched tool, not a menu of theory.
- **TONE:** Collaborative, a little empowering — "here's a move that works." Never lecturing; the science is one plain line, not a lesson.
- **WHAT TO DO (every module):**
  1. **Science context** — one plain-language line on *why* this move works (direction: a real mechanism, no jargon, no citations, ≤1 line — e.g. why naming a need out loud lands better than hinting). This warms the tool; it does not diagnose or advise a life decision.
  2. **The component** — the module's specific UI (buttons / chips / text) for her to do the work.
- **AI JOB (every module):** The science line is SCRIPTED (fixed, vetted copy — never model-invented facts). The *work* inside the component is where an AI job may fire (DRAFT / REFRAME / REFLECT), always with a scripted fallback. **Message modules use DRAFT and she edits + sends — the moon never sends.**
- **REWARD (every module):** **Yes, on completion** — `recordLight`, warm, affirming she practiced the handling (drafted the message, framed the ask, set the boundary). Reward the *doing*, once per module. Then → Time-it (07).

## Module map (each = science line + component)

Each row is a full brief in shorthand; all inherit 6.0's tone, the "science line is scripted" rule, the "edits + sends, never auto-send" rule, and the completion reward.

### 6.1 — Sad / swings
- **ROLE:** Help her hold a low or swinging mood without spiraling on it.
- **WHAT TO DO:** Science line on how mood colors thought (plainly). Offer a few gentle framings/choices; the moon adds one warm line.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** REFRAME a given read (warm the meaning she's holding). Component = buttons + moon line. **Fallback:** scripted framings.

### 6.2 — Anxious
- **ROLE:** Get the worry out of the loop and onto something workable.
- **WHAT TO DO:** Science line on why naming the specific fear shrinks it. She types the worry; chips help her sort/label it.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** REFLECT her worry back / CLASSIFY into chips. Component = text field + chips. **Fallback:** scripted chip set, no reflection.

### 6.3 — Shame
- **ROLE:** Separate "I did a bad thing" from "I am bad" — the core shame-vs-guilt move.
- **WHAT TO DO:** Science line on that split (plain, non-clinical). She writes the thought; buttons offer the kinder, truer read.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** REFRAME the given self-judgment. Component = text field + buttons. **Fallback:** scripted reframes. Guardrail: no diagnosis, never "you're overreacting".

### 6.4 — Numb
- **ROLE:** Gently find the edge of a feeling under the flatness (pairs with the low-arousal lane).
- **WHAT TO DO:** Science line on numbness as protection, not absence. Buttons to approach lightly; a text field if a word surfaces.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** REFLECT anything she offers. Component = buttons + text. **Fallback:** scripted, buttons-only.

### 6.5 — Lonely
- **ROLE:** Turn isolation into one small reach toward a person.
- **WHAT TO DO:** Science line on why a tiny reach beats waiting to feel better first. Fill-chips scaffold a low-stakes message; she edits the text and sends it herself.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** DRAFT (fill-chips → message). Component = fill chips + editable text; **she edits + sends.** **Fallback:** scripted message templates.

### 6.6 — You-hurt-someone / repair
- **ROLE:** Help her make a clean repair without over-apologizing or defending.
- **WHAT TO DO:** Science line on what makes a repair land (own it, no "but"). Pick-to-fill chips build the repair; she edits + sends.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** DRAFT (pick-to-fill → repair message). Component = pick-to-fill chips + editable text; **she sends.** **Fallback:** scripted repair skeleton.

### 6.7 — A real hurt / honest talk
- **ROLE:** Prepare her to raise a genuine hurt directly (the "Real" exit from the mixed lane routes here).
- **WHAT TO DO:** Science line on "I feel __ when __" landing better than blame. She fills the frame, then **rehearses** it (not necessarily sent — this may be spoken in person).
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** DRAFT the "I feel __ when __" line for rehearsal. Component = fill frame + rehearse view. **Fallback:** scripted frame. Guardrail: never dismiss a Real hurt as a swing.

### 6.8 — Work clash / DESC
- **ROLE:** Script a hard work conversation with the DESC structure (Describe · Express · Specify · Consequences).
- **WHAT TO DO:** Science line on why DESC de-escalates. She fills each DESC slot; the moon drafts connective text.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** DRAFT (fill → DESC script). Component = fill fields + text; **she edits + sends/uses.** **Fallback:** scripted DESC template.

### 6.9 — Promotion / frame the ask
- **ROLE:** Help her ask for the raise/role with evidence, not apology.
- **WHAT TO DO:** Science line on leading with contribution, not need. Buttons build the framing; a checklist preps what to bring.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** REFRAME + light DRAFT of the ask. Component = buttons + checklist. **Fallback:** scripted framing + checklist.

### 6.10 — Bias / boundary
- **ROLE:** Help her set a boundary or name a bias cleanly and safely.
- **WHAT TO DO:** Science line on naming behavior + the line, calmly. Buttons pick the boundary shape; a checklist covers protecting herself (document, who to tell).
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** REFRAME/DRAFT the boundary line. Component = buttons + checklist. **Fallback:** scripted boundary phrasings. Guardrail: no legal/HR advice — surface options, she decides.

---

**Auto-send guardrail (repeat, because it matters):** any module that produces text (6.5, 6.6, 6.7, 6.8, 6.9, 6.10) puts the draft in an **editable field she controls**. The moon drafts; **she** edits and **she** sends. No message ever leaves without her explicit send.
