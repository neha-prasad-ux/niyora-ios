# Phase 05 — Mixed / labile lane (all over the place, rejection-sensitive)

Routed here **silently** from her 1.2 feeling. Her emotions are swinging fast — up, down, "he's pulling away", "everyone's upset with me" — often rejection-sensitivity firing. The job isn't to calm *or* activate; it's to help her **not trust the peaks**, tell a swing from a real signal, and anchor. The core AI move is a careful REFLECT of the specific thought she's spinning on. See `_legend.md` for schema + invariants.

---

## Step 5.1 — "Name the swing, trust no peak"

- **ROLE:** Give her a frame for what's happening — the intensity is a swing, and swings lie at their peaks. Naming it as a swing already loosens its grip.
- **TONE:** Steady, orienting, a little grounding-anchor energy. Warm but firm — the fixed point while she's moving.
- **WHAT TO DO:** Name that she's swinging and that peaks aren't reliable readings (direction: "when it's swinging like this, the high points lie — we don't have to trust them"). One or two lines. Don't dismiss the feeling itself — just its reliability at the extremes.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** SCRIPTED intent; SENTENCE optional with fixed fallback ("this is a swing — and swings lie loudest at the top. we don't have to trust the peak"). Component = moon line.
- **REWARD:** None yet.

---

## Step 5.2 — Ride it + self-validate

- **ROLE:** Teach the skill of letting the wave move through without acting on it, while validating that the *feeling* is real even if the *reading* is distorted.
- **TONE:** Companioning. Riding it *with* her, not instructing from outside.
- **WHAT TO DO:** Invite her to let the swing rise and fall without acting, and validate the emotion as real (direction: separate "the feeling is real" from "the story at the peak is true"). No fixing, no reframe yet — the reframe waits for the swing/real sort in 5.4.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** REFLECT (validate her state back) with a scripted fallback ("you're allowed to feel all of this — you just don't have to move on it yet"). Component = moon line, maybe a brief "let it pass" visual beat (no timer, no breath — this isn't the high lane).
- **REWARD:** None yet.

---

## Step 5.3 — "Check the read" (reflect her specific thought back)

- **ROLE:** The pivot of this lane. Surface the exact catastrophic thought she's holding ("he's pulling away", "everyone's upset with me") so it can be examined instead of obeyed.
- **TONE:** Gentle, precise, non-judgmental. Holding the thought up to the light *with* her, not correcting her.
- **WHAT TO DO:** Reflect her specific rejection-thought back in her words (direction: mirror the exact worry she voiced, e.g. "the read right now is: he's pulling away from you"). Must be *her* thought — don't generalize it, don't add a reassurance yet. This sets up the swing/real fork.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** REFLECT (her specific thought, her words). Component = moon line naming the read + the Swing/Real control. **Fallback:** if the model can't safely restate her specific thought, a scripted general form ("the read right now is that something's wrong between you and someone") — never invents a name or a detail she didn't give.
- **REWARD:** None yet.

---

## Step 5.4 — Swing or Real? → branch (de-weight vs don't-dismiss)

- **ROLE:** Sort the reflected thought into a distortion-driven swing or a genuine signal — and respond *oppositely* to each. This is the anti-gaslighting safeguard: real hurts are never waved off as "just a swing".
- **TONE:** Collaborative and respectful of her read. She's the authority on which it is.
- **WHAT TO DO:** Offer two paths on the thought from 5.3:
  - **Swing** → de-weight it: name that this is the sensitivity talking right now, and it doesn't need a response while it's peaking. Direction: lighten its authority, don't deny the feeling.
  - **Real** → **do not dismiss.** Honor that it's a genuine signal worth addressing, and route it toward the matching Act 2 module (e.g. a-real-hurt / honest-talk, lonely, repair). Direction: validate first, then hand to the tool that helps her act on it well.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** CLASSIFY assists (swing-vs-real likelihood) but **she** makes the call via the control. Component = Swing / Real two-way. **Fallback:** if the model can't assist, present the neutral two-way with no lean — her pick governs. Guardrail: never auto-label a "Real" hurt as a swing.
- **REWARD:** None here — reward comes at the anchor (5.5) or downstream in the Act 2 module she's routed to.

---

## Step 5.5 — Anchor

- **ROLE:** Leave her on something stable — a fixed point that outlasts the swing — before the flow moves on.
- **TONE:** Settling, warm, quietly certain. The ground under the wave.
- **WHAT TO DO:** Offer one steadying anchor (direction: a true, stable line she can hold — her worth isn't set by the peak; the swing passes). Then either reward and move to Act 2 (if she wanted to explore) or offer Close. One or two lines.
- **AI JOB + COMPONENT:** REFRAME (warm the given situation into a steadier read) with a scripted fallback ("this passes — it always has. you're okay in the meantime"). Component = moon line + reward bloom, then hands to Act 2 (06) or Close.
- **REWARD:** **Yes** — `recordLight`, calm and steadying. Affirms she rode a swing without acting on the peak, which is the whole skill.
